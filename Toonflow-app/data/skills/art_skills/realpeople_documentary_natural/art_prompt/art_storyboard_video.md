|------|----------|
| **通用多参模式（英文）** | `observational live-action documentary, real human subjects, available natural light, authentic skin texture, unstaged everyday behavior, restrained handheld camera` |
| **通用首尾帧模式（英文）** | `observational live-action documentary, real human subjects, available natural light, authentic skin texture, unstaged everyday behavior, restrained handheld camera, consistent exposure` |
| **Seedance 2.0（中文）** | `真人纪实摄影，真实人物与生活行为，可用自然光，真实肤质，非摆拍现场感，克制手持跟拍` |
